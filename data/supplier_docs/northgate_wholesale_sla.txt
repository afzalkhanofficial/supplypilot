SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT
Northgate Wholesale — SupplyPilot Operations
Agreement Reference: NGW-SLA-2024-002
Effective Date: 1 April 2024

================================================================================
PARTIES
================================================================================

Supplier:  Northgate Wholesale plc, 55 Industrial Ring Road, Leeds, LS12 6EQ,
           United Kingdom ("Northgate")

Customer:  SupplyPilot Operations GmbH, 9 Warehouse District, Hamburg, Germany
           ("Customer")

================================================================================
1. SERVICES COVERED
================================================================================

This SLA covers the supply of the following goods by Northgate to the Customer:
  - Health and beauty accessories
  - Vitamins, supplements, and OTC healthcare products
  - Baby and infant care products

================================================================================
2. LEAD TIMES
================================================================================

2.1 Standard lead time: three (3) business days from order confirmation.

2.2 Healthcare and OTC products subject to regulatory batch release:
    add two (2) additional business days.

2.3 Emergency replenishment:
    Available for products with on-hand cover below 3 days. Emergency orders
    are dispatched within 36 hours. Emergency surcharge: 12% of order value.
    Maximum of four (4) emergency orders per rolling 90 days.

================================================================================
3. FILL RATE AND AVAILABILITY
================================================================================

3.1 Target fill rate: 97% on a rolling 28-day basis.

3.2 Northgate must notify the Customer within 1 business day if availability
    for any contracted SKU falls below 80%. The notification must include:
      - Estimated back-in-stock date
      - Quantity available for partial fulfilment
      - Recommended substitute SKUs (where applicable)

3.3 Chronic shortage (>14 consecutive days below 80% availability) triggers
    a mandatory supplier review meeting within 5 business days.

================================================================================
4. QUALITY AND COMPLIANCE
================================================================================

4.1 All OTC healthcare products must carry valid UK MHRA (or EU equivalent)
    product authorisation where legally required.

4.2 Northgate maintains a GFSI-recognised food safety management certification
    for any food-adjacent storage areas.

4.3 Batch traceability: Northgate shall retain and provide batch/lot records
    for a minimum of 5 years post-delivery, consistent with recall obligations.

4.4 Product Recall: In the event of a voluntary or mandatory product recall,
    Northgate shall notify the Customer within 2 hours of the recall decision
    and provide batch details sufficient for the Customer to identify affected
    stock within its own inventory.

================================================================================
5. PACKAGING AND LABELLING
================================================================================

5.1 All outer packaging must display:
      - Northgate's article number and Customer's purchase order number
      - Country of origin
      - Batch/lot number and expiry date (where applicable)
      - Quantity per carton

5.2 Deviations from labelling requirements must be disclosed at order
    confirmation. Unlabelled or mislabelled goods may be refused at goods-in
    at the Customer's discretion, with return freight at Northgate's expense.

================================================================================
6. PENALTIES
================================================================================

6.1 Fill Rate Below 92% (28-day rolling):
    Credit of 1.5% of the relevant invoice value per full percentage point
    below 92%, capped at 8% of total affected invoice value.

6.2 Late Delivery (>10% of lines in any calendar month):
    Credit of EUR 200 per late delivery event above the 10% threshold.

6.3 Recall Notification Delay (>2 hours):
    EUR 500 per hour of delay beyond the 2-hour notification SLA.

================================================================================
7. FORECASTING AND COLLABORATION
================================================================================

7.1 The Customer provides a rolling 8-week demand forecast to Northgate every
    Monday. Northgate uses this for production and procurement planning only.

7.2 Northgate participates in quarterly business reviews (QBR) with the
    Customer's supply chain team. QBRs are scheduled by the Customer.

================================================================================
8. TERM
================================================================================

Initial term: 12 months from the Effective Date. Auto-renews for 12-month
periods. Either party may exit with 60 days' written notice before renewal.
