================================================================================
PURPOSE
================================================================================

This Policy document sets out Meridian Distributors' ("Meridian") standard
operating procedures for order fulfilment, delivery, returns, and escalation.
It applies to all customers with active supply agreements, including
SupplyPilot Operations GmbH.

Customers are responsible for ensuring their procurement and warehouse teams
are familiar with the procedures set out in this document.

================================================================================
SECTION 1 — ORDER PLACEMENT AND CONFIRMATION
================================================================================

1.1 Accepted Channels
    Orders must be placed through one of the following channels:
      (a) Meridian Supplier Portal (preferred) — https://portal.meridian-dist.com
      (b) EDI 850 purchase order transmission
      (c) Email to orders@meridian-dist.com (manual review, not recommended
          for time-sensitive orders)

1.2 Order Confirmation
    Meridian will confirm all orders within 4 business hours during standard
    business hours (Monday–Friday, 08:00–18:00 CET). Orders placed outside
    business hours will be confirmed by 10:00 CET on the next business day.

1.3 Order Amendments
    Amendments to confirmed orders are accepted within 2 hours of confirmation
    at no charge. Amendments requested after this window are subject to a
    EUR 35 administrative fee per amended line.

1.4 Order Cancellations
    - Within 24 hours of confirmation: no cancellation fee.
    - 24–72 hours after confirmation: 5% of cancelled order value.
    - After 72 hours (if goods are in production or allocated): 15% fee.

================================================================================
SECTION 3 — DELIVERY AND LOGISTICS
================================================================================

3.1 Meridian uses a dedicated carrier network for all deliveries within the
    EU. Third-party carrier details are available upon request.

3.2 Delivery Documentation
    Each shipment is accompanied by:
      - Packing list (SKU, quantity, batch/lot number per line)
      - Certificate of Conformity (where applicable)
      - Carrier waybill with track-and-trace reference

3.3 Proof of Delivery (POD)
    Electronic POD is available in the Meridian Supplier Portal within 2
    business hours of delivery completion.

3.4 Failed Delivery Attempts
    If a delivery cannot be completed (site closed, access denied, etc.),
    Meridian's carrier will attempt re-delivery once at no charge. Subsequent
    attempts are billed at EUR 45 per attempt.

3.5 Delivery Windows
    SupplyPilot's Hamburg DC accepts deliveries Monday–Saturday, 06:00–18:00.
    Saturday deliveries must be pre-booked by Thursday 12:00 CET.

================================================================================
SECTION 4 — RECEIVING AND INSPECTION
================================================================================

4.1 SupplyPilot must inspect all incoming goods within 48 hours of delivery.

4.2 Visible damage or obvious short-delivery must be noted on the carrier's
    delivery receipt at the time of delivery. Failure to note visible issues
    at the point of delivery may limit subsequent claim rights.

4.3 Concealed damage or quality issues discovered post-receipt must be reported
    to Meridian within 5 business days of delivery.

================================================================================
SECTION 5 — RETURNS PROCEDURE
================================================================================

5.1 Authorised Returns
    Returns are accepted in the following circumstances:
      (a) Goods do not meet agreed specification (full credit)
      (b) Transit damage attributable to Meridian or its carrier (full credit)
      (c) Incorrect goods delivered against the purchase order (full credit + 
          re-delivery at no charge)
      (d) Overstock returns by agreement (see 5.3)

5.2 Returns Authorisation (RA)
    All returns require a Returns Authorisation number issued by Meridian.
    To request an RA:
      1. Log into the Meridian Supplier Portal
      2. Select Order → Request Return
      3. Attach supporting evidence (photos, GRN, batch number)
    RA numbers are issued within 2 business days.

5.3 Overstock Returns
    SupplyPilot may return surplus stock under the following conditions:
      - Written request submitted to Meridian at least 14 days in advance
      - Stock must be in original, unopened packaging
      - Shelf life remaining must exceed 50% of total stated shelf life
      - Credit issued: 70% of original invoice price
      - Meridian does not collect overstock returns; SupplyPilot must arrange
        and pay for return freight

5.4 Non-Returnable Items
    The following categories are non-returnable unless defective:
      - Custom-label or customer-specific goods
      - Chilled or temperature-controlled products (once delivered)
      - Promotional and clearance lines

SECTION 6 — ESCALATION PROCEDURE
================================================================================

6.1 Level 1 — Operational Issues
    Contact: Meridian Customer Service
    Email: cs@meridian-dist.com  |  Phone: +31 10 555 0192
    Response SLA: within 4 business hours
    Covers: order status, delivery queries, RA requests, invoicing questions

6.2 Level 2 — Recurring Service Failures
    Contact: SupplyPilot Account Manager at Meridian
    Trigger: same type of issue recurring 3+ times in a 30-day window
    Response SLA: dedicated call within 2 business days
    Covers: KPI miss root-cause analysis, corrective action plans

6.3 Level 3 — Strategic or Contractual Disputes
    Contact: Meridian Head of Key Accounts
    Trigger: unresolved Level 2 issue after 10 business days, or any
    dispute involving contract interpretation or penalty clauses
    Response SLA: senior meeting within 5 business days

================================================================================
SECTION 7 — INVOICE DISPUTES
================================================================================

7.1 SupplyPilot must raise invoice disputes within 15 calendar days of the
    invoice date. Disputes submitted after this window are considered accepted
    unless a material error is proven.

7.2 Disputed amounts should be withheld from payment only to the extent of
    the disputed portion. Undisputed amounts remain payable by the due date.

7.3 Meridian will respond to all invoice disputes within 5 business days.
