SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT
Apex Supply Co. — SupplyPilot Operations
Effective Date: 1 January 2024
Agreement Reference: APEX-SLA-2024-001

================================================================================
PARTIES
================================================================================

This Service Level Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between:

Supplier:  Apex Supply Co., 14 Commerce Park Way, Chicago, IL 60601
           ("Apex" or "Supplier")

Customer:  SupplyPilot Operations GmbH, 9 Warehouse District, Hamburg, Germany
           ("Customer")

================================================================================
1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
================================================================================

Apex Supply Co. agrees to supply the following product categories under the
terms of this Agreement:

  - Consumer packaged goods (CPG) — ambient storage
  - Household and cleaning products
  - Seasonal promotional inventory

All orders placed through the SupplyPilot procurement platform are governed by
this Agreement unless superseded by a separately executed purchase order with
explicit written amendment.

================================================================================
2. LEAD TIME COMMITMENTS
================================================================================

2.1 Standard Orders
    Orders placed Monday through Friday before 14:00 CET shall be dispatched
    within two (2) business days.

2.2 Expedited Orders
    Expedited fulfilment (dispatch within 24 hours) is available for orders
    above EUR 10,000 in value. The expedited surcharge is 8% of order value.

2.3 Large-Volume Orders
    Orders exceeding 5,000 units of a single SKU require a minimum five (5)
    business day lead time for production scheduling.

2.4 Force Majeure
    Lead time commitments are suspended during declared force majeure events
    (see Section 9). Apex will notify the Customer within 4 business hours
    of any force majeure declaration.

================================================================================
3. FILL RATE AND SERVICE LEVEL TARGETS
================================================================================

3.1 Minimum Fill Rate
    Apex commits to a minimum order fill rate of 95% measured on a rolling
    30-day calendar basis. Fill rate is defined as:

        Fill Rate = (Units Shipped / Units Ordered) × 100

3.2 On-Time Delivery
    A minimum of 92% of orders (by line-item count) shall arrive at the
    Customer's designated warehouse within the committed lead time window.

3.3 Measurement and Reporting
    Apex shall provide a monthly service-level report by the 5th business day
    of the following month. The report shall include fill rate, on-time
    delivery rate, and any root-cause analysis for misses exceeding 2%.

================================================================================
4. SUBSTITUTIONS AND BACK-ORDERS
================================================================================

4.1 Apex may substitute an equivalent SKU (same category, ±5% unit cost) only
    with prior written approval from the Customer's procurement contact.

4.2 Back-order notifications must be communicated within 1 business day of
    order receipt. Back-ordered lines must carry an estimated availability date.

4.3 The Customer reserves the right to cancel any back-ordered line that
    cannot be fulfilled within 10 business days of the original order date,
    without penalty.

================================================================================
5. PRICING AND PAYMENT TERMS
================================================================================

5.1 Prices are fixed for the duration of the Agreement unless raw material
    indices (as published by the EU HICP sub-index for industrial goods)
    shift by more than 8% over any rolling 90-day window.

5.2 Price revision requests must be submitted with supporting documentation
    at least 30 calendar days before the requested effective date.

5.3 Payment terms: net-30 from the date of a valid invoice. Early payment
    discount of 1.5% is available for payment within 10 days.

5.4 Invoices must reference the SupplyPilot purchase order number and include
    batch/lot numbers for all perishable or serialised goods.

================================================================================
6. QUALITY STANDARDS
================================================================================

6.1 All goods must conform to the product specifications agreed in Schedule A
    (attached separately).

6.2 Apex shall maintain ISO 9001:2015 certification throughout the term of this
    Agreement. Copies of current certification shall be provided upon request.

6.3 Shelf-Life Minimum: perishable products must arrive with no less than 75%
    of their stated shelf life remaining.

6.4 Apex accepts returns for:
    - Products that fail to meet specification (full credit, no restocking fee)
    - Products damaged in transit attributable to Apex's carrier (full credit)
    - Surplus stock above agreed maximum inventory days (50% credit, within
      90 days of delivery, maximum 10% of invoice value per order)

================================================================================
7. PENALTIES AND REMEDIES
================================================================================

7.1 Fill Rate Miss: If the rolling 30-day fill rate falls below 90%, Apex
    shall provide a credit of 2% of the affected invoice value per percentage
    point below 90%, up to a maximum of 10%.

7.2 Late Delivery: If on-time delivery falls below 85% in any calendar month,
    the Customer may issue a performance improvement notice (PIN). If the next
    month's on-time rate remains below 85%, the Customer may terminate this
    Agreement with 30 days' notice, without penalty.

7.3 Liability Cap: Apex's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall
    not exceed the total invoice value paid by the Customer in the preceding
    12-month period.

================================================================================
8. FORECASTING AND DEMAND SHARING
================================================================================

8.1 The Customer shall share a rolling 13-week demand forecast via the
    SupplyPilot API no later than every Monday by 09:00 CET.

8.2 Apex shall use the forecast for production planning only and shall not
    disclose forecast data to third parties.

8.3 Significant forecast deviations (>30% from prior week) shall be flagged
    by the Customer with a written explanation within 24 hours.

================================================================================
9. FORCE MAJEURE
================================================================================

Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform obligations under this
Agreement if such failure results from: natural disaster, pandemic, government
embargo, port closure, or other events beyond the reasonable control of the
affected party. The affected party must notify the other within 4 business
hours and provide updates every 48 hours until resolution.

================================================================================
10. TERM AND TERMINATION
================================================================================

10.1 Initial term: 12 months from the Effective Date, auto-renewing for
     successive 12-month periods unless either party gives 60 days' written
     notice of non-renewal.

10.2 Either party may terminate for material breach with 30 days' written
     notice if the breach is not cured within the notice period.
