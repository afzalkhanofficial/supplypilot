SUPPLY AND DISTRIBUTION CONTRACT
Clearline Logistics — SupplyPilot Operations
Contract Reference: CLL-DIST-2024-007
Execution Date: 15 February 2024

================================================================================
PARTIES
================================================================================

This Supply and Distribution Contract ("Contract") is made between:

Supplier:  Clearline Logistics Ltd., Unit 8, Gateway Industrial Estate,
           Rotterdam, Netherlands ("Clearline")

Customer:  SupplyPilot Operations GmbH, 9 Warehouse District, Hamburg, Germany
           ("SupplyPilot" or "Customer")

================================================================================
ARTICLE 1 — SUBJECT MATTER
================================================================================

1.1 Clearline agrees to provide warehousing, pick-and-pack, and last-mile
    distribution services for the Customer's inventory of fast-moving consumer
    goods (FMCG) as specified in Annex I (Product List).

1.2 Clearline additionally agrees to supply the following product categories
    directly from its own stock:

      Category A — Ambient grocery lines (canned goods, dry staples)
      Category B — Personal care and hygiene products
      Category C — Non-food household items

================================================================================
ARTICLE 2 — MINIMUM ORDER QUANTITIES (MOQ)
================================================================================

2.1 Category A: MOQ of 200 units per SKU per order.
2.2 Category B: MOQ of 150 units per SKU per order.
2.3 Category C: MOQ of 100 units per SKU per order.

2.4 Orders below MOQ will be fulfilled at a 12% above-catalogue surcharge.
    SupplyPilot must explicitly approve this surcharge before order confirmation.

================================================================================
ARTICLE 3 — DELIVERY SCHEDULES AND LEAD TIMES
================================================================================

3.1 Standard Lead Time: four (4) business days from confirmed purchase order.

3.2 Clearline operates fixed delivery windows into SupplyPilot's Hamburg
    distribution centre:
      - Tuesday and Thursday: 06:00–12:00 CET
      - Weekend deliveries are available by prior arrangement at a 15% surcharge.

3.3 Order cut-off times:
      - Tuesday window: orders confirmed by Friday 17:00 CET
      - Thursday window: orders confirmed by Tuesday 17:00 CET

3.4 Any delivery falling more than 2 hours outside the agreed window without
    prior notification by Clearline shall be classified as a late delivery for
    service-level measurement purposes.

================================================================================
ARTICLE 4 — INVENTORY CONSIGNMENT AND HOLDING
================================================================================

4.1 Clearline shall maintain a safety buffer of 14 days' average demand
    (based on the prior 4-week average) for all Category A and B lines.

4.2 Clearline shall notify SupplyPilot when on-hand stock for any SKU falls
    below 7 days of cover. Notification must arrive within 1 business day of
    the cover threshold being breached.

4.3 Excess inventory (stock exceeding 60 days of cover) may be returned by
    SupplyPilot with 10 days' written notice, subject to a 5% restocking fee.

================================================================================
ARTICLE 5 — COLD CHAIN AND HANDLING
================================================================================

5.1 All ambient-category products must be stored at 10–25 °C with relative
    humidity below 70%.

5.2 Temperature excursions exceeding these limits must be documented and
    disclosed within 4 hours. SupplyPilot reserves the right to reject affected
    lots at no cost.

5.3 Clearline shall maintain HACCP compliance certificates for all storage
    facilities handling food-adjacent products. Certificates are due for renewal
    annually and copies provided to SupplyPilot upon request.

================================================================================
ARTICLE 6 — CLAIMS AND RETURNS PROCEDURE
================================================================================

6.1 Claims for short-delivery, damage, or non-conforming goods must be
    submitted by SupplyPilot within 5 business days of receipt.

6.2 Claims must be accompanied by:
      (a) Goods Received Note (GRN) signed by SupplyPilot's warehouse team
      (b) Photographic evidence for damaged goods
      (c) Clearline's delivery reference number

6.3 Clearline shall acknowledge claims within 2 business days and resolve them
    (credit or replacement) within 10 business days.

================================================================================
ARTICLE 7 — PRICING AND COST ESCALATION
================================================================================

7.1 Unit prices are as listed in Annex II (Price Schedule), valid for 12 months
    from the execution date.

7.2 Clearline may request a price review with 45 calendar days' written notice,
    supported by documented cost evidence (fuel index, labour statistics).

7.3 Annual price increases are capped at the lesser of (a) 5% or (b) the
    European Transport Cost Index (ETCI) change over the preceding 12 months.

7.4 Payment terms: net-45 from invoice date. Clearline may charge statutory
    late-payment interest (EU Directive 2011/7/EU) on overdue invoices.

================================================================================
ARTICLE 8 — KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs)
================================================================================

Clearline is measured monthly on the following KPIs:

  KPI                       Target        Minimum Threshold
  ──────────────────────    ──────────    ─────────────────
  On-time delivery rate     ≥ 95%         88%
  Order accuracy (lines)    ≥ 99%         97%
  Damage-free rate          ≥ 99.5%       98.5%
  Claim resolution time     ≤ 10 days     ≤ 15 days

If Clearline misses the Minimum Threshold on two or more KPIs in the same
calendar month, SupplyPilot may issue a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
Failure to meet targets within 60 days of a PIP triggers a termination right.

================================================================================
ARTICLE 9 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND CONFIDENTIALITY
================================================================================

9.1 All demand forecasts, inventory data, and commercial terms shared by
    SupplyPilot with Clearline are strictly confidential and may not be
    disclosed to any third party without prior written consent.

9.2 Clearline shall implement reasonable data security measures equivalent to
    ISO 27001 standards and notify SupplyPilot of any data breach within
    72 hours of discovery.

================================================================================
ARTICLE 10 — GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
================================================================================

10.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of the Netherlands.

10.2 Disputes shall be referred first to senior management negotiation (30 days),
     then to mediation under the ICC Mediation Rules (30 days), and finally to
     arbitration under the ICC Rules with the seat in Amsterdam.

================================================================================
ARTICLE 11 — TERM
================================================================================

11.1 This Contract commences on the Execution Date and continues for 24 months.

11.2 Renewal: automatic 12-month renewal unless either party provides 90 days'
     written notice of termination before the expiry of the current term.

================================================================================
ANNEX I — PRODUCT LIST (summary)
================================================================================

Clearline holds contracted stock for SKUs 1–7 (SupplyPilot internal reference).
Full SKU list and quantities maintained in the SupplyPilot procurement system.
