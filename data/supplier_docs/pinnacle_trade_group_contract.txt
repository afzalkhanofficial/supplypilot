MASTER SUPPLY CONTRACT
Pinnacle Trade Group — SupplyPilot Operations
Contract Reference: PTG-MSC-2024-011
Execution Date: 10 May 2024

================================================================================
PARTIES
================================================================================

Supplier:  Pinnacle Trade Group B.V., Breda Business Park, Unit 22,
           4825 AA Breda, Netherlands ("Pinnacle")

Customer:  SupplyPilot Operations GmbH, 9 Warehouse District, Hamburg, Germany
           ("SupplyPilot" or "Customer")

================================================================================
ARTICLE 1 — SCOPE
================================================================================

1.1 Pinnacle agrees to supply the following product categories:
      - Electronics accessories (cables, batteries, adaptors)
      - Small domestic appliances
      - Seasonal goods (defined per Annex I, reviewed annually)

1.2 This Contract supersedes all prior purchase orders and letters of intent
    between the parties relating to the categories listed above.

================================================================================
ARTICLE 2 — ORDERING PROCESS
================================================================================

2.1 Purchase orders must be transmitted electronically via the SupplyPilot
    procurement platform or by EDI 850. Email orders are not accepted.

2.2 Pinnacle will confirm or reject each purchase order within 6 business hours.
    Rejection must include the reason and an alternative proposal.

2.3 Blanket orders for a fixed SKU list may be established quarterly. Blanket
    order call-offs (releases) must be placed with a minimum 72-hour notice.

================================================================================
ARTICLE 3 — LEAD TIMES AND DELIVERY
================================================================================

3.1 Standard Lead Times:
      Electronics accessories:  5 business days
      Small domestic appliances: 7 business days
      Seasonal goods:           10 business days (extended to 15 for peak season,
                                  defined as 1 Oct – 31 Dec)

3.2 Lead times are measured from purchase order confirmation, not transmission.

3.3 Pinnacle ships DDP (Delivered Duty Paid, Incoterms 2020) to the Customer's
    Hamburg distribution centre. All import duties and freight costs are borne
    by Pinnacle.

3.4 Pinnacle shall provide tracking information within 1 business day of dispatch.

================================================================================
ARTICLE 4 — VOLUME COMMITMENTS AND FORECASTS
================================================================================

4.1 The Customer commits to a minimum annual purchase volume of EUR 240,000
    across all categories. If this minimum is not met by 31 December of the
    contract year, the Customer shall pay a shortfall fee of 3% of the gap.

4.2 The Customer provides a rolling 12-week demand forecast to Pinnacle every
    Friday by 12:00 CET. Pinnacle is not obligated to reserve capacity beyond
    the forecast quantities.

4.3 Pinnacle may use forecast data solely for internal planning and must not
    share it with competitors or third parties.

================================================================================
ARTICLE 5 — PRICING
================================================================================

5.1 Prices are as specified in Annex II (Price Schedule) and fixed for 12 months.

5.2 After 12 months, Pinnacle may propose a price revision no more than once
    per contract year. The revision request must be supported by documented
    component cost evidence and submitted with 60 days' notice.

5.3 Maximum annual price increase: the lesser of (a) 6% or (b) the Dutch CPI
    change over the preceding 12 months, as published by Statistics Netherlands.

5.4 Volume discounts apply as follows:
      Annual spend EUR 240,000–400,000:    catalogue price
      Annual spend EUR 400,001–600,000:    3% discount on catalogue price
      Annual spend above EUR 600,000:       6% discount on catalogue price

================================================================================
ARTICLE 6 — WARRANTIES AND PRODUCT COMPLIANCE
================================================================================

6.1 All electronics products must carry CE marking and comply with applicable
    EU directives (LVD, EMC, RoHS) at the time of delivery.

6.2 Pinnacle warrants that all goods are free from defects in materials and
    workmanship for 24 months from the date of delivery.

6.3 Warranty claims must be submitted by the Customer within the warranty period
    via the Pinnacle Partner Portal. Pinnacle will respond within 5 business
    days with a resolution (repair, replacement, or credit).

6.4 Pinnacle maintains product liability insurance of EUR 5,000,000 per claim.
    Certificates of insurance are provided annually or upon request.

================================================================================
ARTICLE 7 — RETURNS AND DEFECTS
================================================================================

7.1 Dead-on-arrival (DOA) policy:
      - DOA claims must be submitted within 30 days of delivery.
      - Pinnacle issues a full credit or replacement for confirmed DOA units.
      - DOA rate above 1% of any shipment triggers a mandatory quality review.

7.2 In-warranty failures (post-30-days):
      - Customer returns failed units to Pinnacle (Pinnacle arranges collection
        for batches of 10+ units; smaller quantities sent by tracked post).
      - Pinnacle repairs or replaces within 15 business days.

7.3 End-of-life (EOL) stock buyback:
      - Pinnacle will repurchase unsold EOL units at 40% of original invoice
        price, provided the Customer gives 30 days' written notice.

================================================================================
ARTICLE 8 — PEAK SEASON PROVISIONS
================================================================================

8.1 Peak season is defined as 1 October through 31 December.

8.2 Pinnacle shall confirm peak-season capacity allocation by 1 August each year.
    The Customer must submit peak-season demand forecasts by 15 July.

8.3 Lead times during peak season are extended as noted in Article 3.1.

8.4 No price surcharges apply during peak season; volume discount tiers
    (Article 5.4) continue to apply based on year-to-date spend.

================================================================================
ARTICLE 9 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND GOVERNING LAW
================================================================================

9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of the Netherlands.

9.2 Any dispute shall first be subject to 30 days of good-faith negotiation
    between senior representatives of each party.

9.3 Unresolved disputes are referred to arbitration under the Netherlands
    Arbitration Institute (NAI) Rules, with the seat in Rotterdam.

================================================================================
ARTICLE 10 — TERM AND TERMINATION
================================================================================

10.1 This Contract is effective for 24 months from the Execution Date.

10.2 It renews automatically for 12-month periods unless either party gives
     90 days' written notice before the end of the current term.

10.3 Either party may terminate immediately for insolvency, fraud, or material
     breach not cured within 30 days of written notice.

================================================================================
ANNEX I — SEASONAL GOODS DEFINITION
================================================================================

Seasonal goods are defined annually by mutual agreement before 1 August.
For the 2024 calendar year, seasonal goods include:
  - Christmas lights and decorations (eligible October–December)
  - Summer outdoor accessories (eligible April–August)
  - School stationery kits (eligible July–September)
